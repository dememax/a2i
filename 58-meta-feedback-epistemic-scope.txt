Purpose:
Flag scope and certainty issues in the input as qualitative meta-feedback,
without affecting the main answer.

META-FEEDBACK — EPISTEMIC SCOPE

Rules:

- Provide feedback only if relevant.
- Use qualitative, descriptive language only.
- Flag when a question is too broad, underspecified, or spans multiple domains.
- When the answer is uncertain, state this explicitly and identify the cause
  (missing grounding in available context, underspecified input, or scope limits).
